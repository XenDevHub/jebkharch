🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Jeb Kharch ("we", "our", or "us") is a mobile quiz, trivia, and learning reward platform. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy.

1. Information We Collect

When you register and use Jeb Kharch, we collect the following types of information:

  • Phone Number: Used solely for secure OTP SMS account verification and authentication.
  • Wallet & Disbursement Details: Account number or phone number for processing cash reward payouts via JazzCash, Easypaisa, or bank transfer upon your request.
  • Quiz & Gameplay Activity: Your quiz answers, tournament scores, coins earned, and leaderboard rankings.
  • Device & Anti-Fraud Identifiers: Device model, OS version, and network identifiers collected to prevent cheating, multi-accounting, and fraudulent withdrawal attempts.

2. Advertising & Analytics (Google AdMob)

Jeb Kharch is monetized through advertising provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process device advertising identifiers (Google Advertising ID) to serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements, track ad performance, and combat fraudulent clicks.

You can manage or reset your Advertising ID directly in your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create, manage, and verify your player account via OTP.
  • To calculate quiz scores, rankings, and coin rewards.
  • To disburse cash prize withdrawals to your selected payment provider (JazzCash / Easypaisa).
  • To detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent quiz manipulation and bot activity.
  • To send optional push notifications regarding tournaments and reward statuses.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We only share information with:

  • Payment Gateways & Telecom Partners: To disburse approved wallet withdrawals and deliver SMS OTPs.
  • Google AdMob & Firebase: For ad delivery and cloud messaging infrastructure.
  • Legal Authorities: Only when strictly required by applicable local law or fraud regulations.

5. Data Security & Storage

All communication between the mobile app and our servers is encrypted using industry-standard TLS/SSL (HTTPS). Sensitive database records (e.g. transactions, user balances) are protected behind firewalled cloud infrastructure with automated encrypted backups.

6. Account Deletion & Data Retention

You have the right to request the deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time. To request deletion, you can contact us at the support email provided below. Once verified, your account, phone number, and transaction logs will be permanently deleted from our active database within 30 days.

7. Age Requirement (18+)

Because Jeb Kharch offers financial coin rewards and wallet disbursements, our service is strictly intended for individuals aged 18 years or older. We do not knowingly allow individuals under 18 to participate or collect prizes.
